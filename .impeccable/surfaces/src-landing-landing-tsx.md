# Hearing Assist landing page

Scope: `/`, the public website. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: deaf and hard-of-hearing students (and the people who support them) deciding whether this is worth opening in class. Action: open the app and start captioning. Proof: the product itself at work (captions settling, mode notes, quote-backed answers); true facts only (no account, on-device, WCAG 2.2 AA). No testimonials, logos or accuracy numbers.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is the caption screen at full scale: words of a real-feeling lecture settle into sharp, large type over the aura, the thing a student will actually read. Refuses the split hero (copy left, phone right, badge row) and the three-equal-card feature grid.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink #0e1a16, one green #0f7a4a action, AI blue #0b62c4, red only on the record dot; soft green+blue aura only behind the stage and the closing band. Bricolage Grotesque display at large optical size with tight tracking; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything read.
STORY: A visitor reads one sentence, watches captions settle in big type, sees the same lecture become Lecture/Presentation/Meeting/Seminar notes, tries caption styles, learns it runs in the browser with no account, then taps Start captioning.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky nav (wordmark, four links, Open app). Left-aligned display headline over two lines, lead, green pill CTA + quiet link. Below, a full-width rounded stage (28px, aura) holding a status capsule, timer, waveform and 2-3 large caption lines settling; on desktop a note card peeks from the stage's right edge.
FORM: caption screen as page (own world, approved plan; position 1); seed: none (unavailable)
SIGNATURE: words that settle (blur-to-sharp) in the stage, reused in the live app and notes.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
